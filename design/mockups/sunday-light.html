First Sunday, Design Direction

Sunday Morning Light

The whole app moves through one Sunday morning: the quiz starts before dawn and ends in full light, so progress is felt as the sun coming up, not read off a bar. Fit is an arc of light, not a percentage. Every screen keeps a real person in view, because the light is beautiful but the people are the point.
