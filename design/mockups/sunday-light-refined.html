First Sunday, Design Direction

Sunday Morning Light

The whole app moves through one Sunday morning: the quiz starts before dawn and ends in full light, so progress is felt as the sun coming up, not read off a bar. Fit shows up as a small glow in the corner, a badge of light, not a dashboard gauge, and it is never the first thing you read. The person and the place lead every screen, because the light sets the mood, but the people are the point.
